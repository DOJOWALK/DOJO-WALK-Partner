# ADR-001: Notion as Product Source of Truth

- Status: Accepted as a working project instruction; individual Notion policy approval statuses remain authoritative.
- Context: DOJO WALK requires consistency across Admin, Partner, Customer, Website and backend.
- Decision: Feature behavior, roles, workflows and policy rules must be traced to Notion. Draft policies are not treated as approved production requirements.
- Consequences: Record gaps and undecided rules instead of guessing. Keep UI, client validation, backend enforcement and tests aligned. Any mismatch must be documented for resolution before release.
